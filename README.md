# Faahiye Shoes - Modern E-Commerce & Management Dashboard

Website casri ah oo loogu talagalay iibinta kabaha, ku dhisan **HTML5, Tailwind CSS, iyo JavaScript Dynamic ah**. Waxaa ku jira nidaam buuxa oo dukaameysi (Storefront) iyo Dashboard maamul oo awood badan (Admin Dashboard) oo wata keydinta tooska ah ee **LocalStorage**.

---

## 🌟 Muuqaalada Guud (Key Features)

### 1. Dukaanka Macaamiisha (Storefront)
- **Kala Saarid Casri ah (Categories Filter):** 
  - Dhammaan Kabaha
  - Ragga (Men's Shoes)
  - Dumarka (Women's Shoes & Heels)
  - Ciyaaraha (Sneakers & Running Shoes)
  - Xarrago (Italian Leather & Loafers)
  - Carruurta (Kids Athletic Shoes)
- **Raadinta Tooska ah (Live Search):** Raadi magaca, nooca, ama sifooyinka kabaha ilbiriqsi gudaheed.
- **Kala Saarid Qiime & Qiimeyn (Sorting):** Qiimaha ugu hooseeya, ugu sarreeya, kuwa ugu cusub, ama qiimeynta xiddigaha.
- **Xulashada Cabbirka (Shoe Size Picker):** Dooro cabbirkaaga (36 ilaa 45) ka hor intaadan ku darin gaariga.
- **Eegis Degdeg ah (Quick View Modal):** Daawo sawirka weyn, qiimaha, xaddiga kaydka ku haray, iyo faahfaahinta kasta.
- **Koodhadhka Dhimista (Promo Coupon Codes):**
  - Geli koodhka `FAAHIYE15` si aad u hesho **15% dhimis**
  - Geli koodhka `SOOMAALI10` si aad u hesho **10% dhimis**
- **Gaariga Iibsiga (Dynamic Cart Slide-Over):** Kudar, ka saar, ama kordhi xabbadaha adigoo arkaya wadarta qiimaha oo isbeddelaysa isla markiiba.
- **Dhameystirka Dalabka (Checkout):** 
  - Foom fudud oo loogu talagalay macaamiisha Soomaaliyeed.
  - Taageerada lacag bixinta: **EVC Plus**, **Zaad Service**, **Sahal**, **Kaash marka lagu gaarsiiyo (COD)**, iyo Card.

### 2. Dashboard-ka Maamulka (Admin Dashboard)
- **KPI Metrics & Stats:**
  - Wadarta Dakhliga (Total Revenue)
  - Dalabaadka Guud (Total Orders)
  - Noocyada Kabaha Kaydka (Total Product Varieties)
  - Xabbadaha Kaydka Guud (Total Units in Stock)
- **Shaxda Iibka (Dynamic Sales Chart):** Shax canvas ah oo muujinaysa iibka 7-dii maalmood ee la soo dhaafay.
- **Maamulka Kabaha (Product CRUD):**
  - **Kudar Kaba Cusub (Add Product):** Magac, qayb, qiime, qiimihii hore, sawir URL (ama ku dhufo sawirrada diyaarka ah), cabbirro, iyo faahfaahin.
  - **Wax ka bedel (Edit Product):** Bedel macluumaadka kabaha wakhti kasta.
  - **Tirtir (Delete Product):** Ka saar kabaha aadan rabin.
- **Maamulka Dalabaadka (Order Management):**
  - Eeg dhammaan dalabaadka ay macaamiishu soo direen.
  - U bedel xaaladda dalabka: `Sugaya` (Pending), `La Diray` (Shipped), `La Gaarsiiyay` (Delivered), ama `La Joojiyay` (Cancelled).
- **Dib u Soo Celinta Xogta (Reset Demo Data):** Hal gujin ku soo celi xogtii tusaalaha ahayd haddii aad tirtirto.

## 🚀 Sida Loo Furo (How to Run)

1. Wax rakibid ama server ah uma baahna.
2. Si toos ah u fur faylka `index.html` adigoo laba jeer gujinaya (double-click) ama ku fur browser-kaaga (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
3. Waxaad isla markiiba arki doontaa dukaanka oo shaqeynaya.
4. Si aad u gasho Dashboard-ka, ku dhufo badhanka **"Dashboard"** ee ku yaal geesta sare ee midig.
